Our Story

Bingwa Data was born from a simple observation: Kenyans were paying too much for mobile data and airtime. Official telco rates were eating into people's budgets, and Okoa Jahazi debt made it even harder to stay connected.

We built Bingwa Data to change that. What started as a passion project has grown into Kenya's trusted marketplace for discounted Safaricom and Airtel bundles. Our mission is simple — make internet and communication affordable for every Kenyan, without the usual restrictions.

💡 Why "Bingwa"? In Swahili, Bingwa means "champion" or "expert." We chose this name because we're committed to being champions for Kenyan consumers — helping you get the best value for your money, every single time.
