Understanding how payments work on Bingwa Data is essential for a smooth and fast bundle buying experience. Our payment system is designed to be secure, instant, and hassle-free.

This guide explains everything you need to know about the Bingwa Data payment process – from M-PESA STK Push to manual Till payments and how your bundle is delivered after payment.

Payment Overview

Bingwa Data uses M-PESA as the exclusive payment method for all bundle purchases. We offer two ways to pay:

  • STK Push (Recommended): You receive a payment request on your phone and confirm with your M-PESA PIN. Fastest and most secure.
  • Manual Till Payment: You send money directly to our Till number using the "Lipa na M-PESA" option on your phone.
All payments are processed through Till 1621614. This is the only official Bingwa Data payment channel. Be cautious of any other numbers.

M-PESA STK Push – How It Works

STK Push is the preferred and fastest payment method on Bingwa Data. Here's how it works:

  1. You select a bundle on our website or via WhatsApp.
  2. You enter your phone number (the one registered with M-PESA).
  3. We trigger an M-PESA STK Push payment request to your phone.
  4. You receive a pop-up on your phone asking you to confirm payment.
  5. You enter your M-PESA PIN and confirm.
  6. Payment is processed instantly, and your bundle is delivered.
✅ No need to type the Till number manually. The STK Push handles everything automatically, reducing errors and saving time.

Manual Till Payment

If you prefer to send money manually, you can use the "Lipa na M-PESA" option on your phone:

  1. Open your M-PESA menu on your phone.
  2. Select "Lipa na M-PESA" then "Pay Bill".
  3. Enter 1621614 as the Business Number.
  4. Enter your phone number as the Account Number.
  5. Enter the amount and confirm with your PIN.
Note: Manual payments may take slightly longer to reflect. We recommend STK Push for instant delivery.
M-PESA Till: 1621614

Step-by-Step Payment Flow

Here is a visual breakdown of the complete payment process from start to finish:

1 Choose your bundle – Select data, minutes, or SMS bundle on our website or via WhatsApp.

After Payment – Bundle Delivery

Once payment is confirmed, here's what happens:

  • Instant delivery: Your bundle is sent to your phone within seconds of payment confirmation.
  • Confirmation message: You'll receive an SMS confirming the bundle activation.
  • Balance update: Your bundle balance is updated immediately (check via USSD or your account dashboard).
  • Support: If you don't receive your bundle within 2 minutes, contact us on WhatsApp at 0743731749.
