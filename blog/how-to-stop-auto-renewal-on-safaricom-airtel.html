How to Stop Auto-Renewal on Safaricom

Safaricom offers several ways to manage your subscriptions. Here are the most reliable methods:

Method 1: Via USSD (*544#)

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 3 (My Bundles) or Option 2 (Data Bundles) depending on your menu version.
  3. Select "Manage Subscriptions" or "View Active Bundles".
  4. You'll see a list of your active bundles with auto‑renewal enabled.
  5. Select the bundle you want to stop, then choose "Cancel" or "Stop Auto-Renewal".
  6. Confirm the cancellation. You'll receive a confirmation SMS.

⚡ Quick Reference (Safaricom)

USSD Code*544#
Menu PathOption 3 → Manage Subscriptions → Cancel
Alternative Shortcut*544*3#

Method 2: Via MySafaricom App

  • Open the MySafaricom App and log in.
  • Tap on "Bundles" or "My Subscriptions".
  • You'll see all active bundles with a toggle for auto‑renewal.
  • Turn the toggle OFF for the bundle(s) you want to stop.
  • Confirm the change – the app will show a success message.

How to Stop Auto-Renewal on Airtel

Airtel's process is similar, with slightly different menu options:

Method 1: Via USSD (*544#)

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 2 (Data Bundles).
  3. Select "Manage Subscriptions" or "Active Plans".
  4. Choose the bundle you want to stop auto‑renewal for.
  5. Select "Cancel" or "Stop Auto-Renewal".
  6. Confirm and wait for the SMS confirmation.

⚡ Quick Reference (Airtel)

USSD Code*544#
Menu PathOption 2 → Manage Subscriptions → Cancel
Alternative Shortcut*544*2#

Method 2: Via MyAirtel App

  • Open the MyAirtel App and log in.
  • Go to "Bundles" or "My Plans".
  • Find the active bundle and tap on it.
  • Toggle off "Auto-Renew".
  • Confirm the change.

Using the Apps to Manage Renewals

Both MySafaricom and MyAirtel apps give you a clear dashboard of all your active subscriptions. Here's why using the app is better:

What Happens After You Stop Auto-Renewal?

  • Your current data remains active until the validity period ends. For example, if you have a daily bundle that renews at midnight, cancelling before renewal means you keep the remaining data for the day.
  • No further deductions will be made from your airtime for that bundle.
  • You can always manually buy the bundle again later via USSD or from platforms like Bingwa Data.

💡 Tip: If you want to keep using the bundle occasionally, write down the USSD code for manual purchase so you can reactivate it quickly when needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop auto-renewal on Safaricom bundles?

You can stop auto-renewal on Safaricom by dialing *544# and selecting 'Manage Subscriptions' or 'My Bundles' to see your active plans and cancel auto-renewal. Alternatively, use the MySafaricom App to turn off auto-renewal for each bundle.

How do I stop auto-renewal on Airtel bundles?

For Airtel, dial *544# and choose 'Data Bundles' then 'Manage Subscriptions' to cancel auto-renewal. You can also use the MyAirtel App to disable auto-renewal for daily, weekly, or monthly plans.

Can I stop auto-renewal without losing my remaining data?

Yes. Cancelling auto-renewal only stops the next automatic purchase. You keep all unused data until the current validity period expires. You can manually buy a new bundle later when you need it.

Why should I stop auto-renewal on bundles?

Auto-renewal can lead to unnecessary charges if you don't use the data. By stopping it, you only buy bundles when you actually need them, saving money. Also, you can compare cheaper deals from platforms like Bingwa Data before renewing.
