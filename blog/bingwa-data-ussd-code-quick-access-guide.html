Did you know you can access Bingwa Data services without an internet connection? Our USSD code allows you to check your balance, buy bundles, and even gift data – all from your phone's dialer.

This guide explains everything you need to know about the Bingwa Data USSD code, how to use it, and what you can do with it.

What is the Bingwa Data USSD Code?

USSD (Unstructured Supplementary Service Data) is a technology that lets you interact with our system via a shortcode. It works on any phone, even basic feature phones, and doesn't require data or Wi-Fi.

*384#
Bingwa Data USSD Shortcode (example – check active code on website)
Note: The exact USSD code may be updated. Always confirm the current code from our homepage or by contacting our support.

How to Dial the USSD Code

  1. Open your phone's dialer (the app you use to make calls).
  2. Type *384# (or the active shortcode).
  3. Press the call button (green button).
  4. A menu will appear on your screen. Follow the prompts to navigate.
  5. Select the service you want (e.g., Buy Bundle, Check Balance, Gift Data).
  6. Enter the required details (e.g., bundle type, phone number) and confirm.
  7. You will receive a confirmation message, and your bundle will be delivered instantly.
Tip: Make sure you have sufficient M‑PESA balance if you are purchasing a bundle via USSD.

Services Available via USSD

With the Bingwa Data USSD code, you can:

  • Check balance: See how much data, minutes, or SMS you have left.
  • Buy bundles: Purchase data, minutes, or SMS bundles directly.
  • Gift bundles: Send a bundle to another Safaricom or Airtel number.
  • View active bundles: See all your current active subscriptions.
  • Get support: Access help and contact information.

🎁 Gift a bundle via USSD! It's a great way to surprise someone with data or minutes.

USSD vs. Online – Which Should You Use?

  • USSD – Best when you have no internet, need quick access, or are using a feature phone. Works on any network.
  • Website (datasokoni.com) – Offers more detailed product info, easier navigation, and visual bundle comparisons. Recommended when you have internet.

Both methods are secure and provide instant delivery. Choose the one that suits your situation.
