Welcome to Bingwa Data! Before you can start buying bundles and enjoying our low prices, you need to create an account. Registration is quick, free, and takes just a few minutes.

This guide walks you through the entire registration process – whether you prefer to register online via our website or using the USSD code from your phone.

Why Register?

  • Faster checkout: Save your phone number and preferences for one-click purchases.
  • Order history: View all your past purchases and bundle usage.
  • Gift management: Easily send bundles to friends and family.
  • Support: Our team can assist you faster when you have an account.
  • Exclusive offers: Get notified about special promotions and discounts.

Online Registration (Website)

Follow these steps to register on the Bingwa Data website:

  1. Visit our website: Go to datasokoni.com.
  2. Click 'Register' or 'Sign Up': Find the registration link at the top right corner of the page.
  3. Fill in your details:
    • Full name
    • Phone number (Safaricom or Airtel)
    • Email address
    • Create a password
  4. Verify your phone: You will receive an SMS with a verification code. Enter it to confirm your number.
  5. Accept terms: Read and agree to our Terms of Service.
  6. Click 'Create Account': Your account is now ready!
Tip: Use a valid phone number – this is where we'll deliver your bundles.

USSD Registration (Phone)

If you don't have internet access, you can register via USSD:

  1. Dial the USSD code: On your phone, dial *384# (or the active shortcode).
  2. Select 'Register': Follow the menu prompts to choose registration.
  3. Enter your details: Provide your name and other required information.
  4. Confirm: Review your details and confirm to complete registration.
  5. Receive confirmation: You'll get an SMS confirming your account creation.
Note: USSD registration requires you to have a valid M‑PESA number associated with your phone.

After Registration – What Next?

Once your account is created, you can:

  • Log in to your account on the website or via USSD.
  • Browse bundles and choose the best plan for your needs.
  • Buy bundles using M‑PESA Till 1621614.
  • Gift bundles to any Safaricom or Airtel number.
  • Check your balance anytime to track your usage.
