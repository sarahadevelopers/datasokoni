STK Push Payment (Recommended)

STK Push is the preferred payment method because it's faster and more secure. Here's how it works:

  1. Place your order: Select your minutes bundle on our website, via WhatsApp, or via SMS.
  2. Enter your phone number: Provide the M-PESA registered number where you want the payment request sent.
  3. Receive the STK Push: You'll get a pop-up notification on your phone from M-PESA asking you to confirm a payment.
  4. Enter your PIN: Type your M-PESA PIN to authorize the transaction.
  5. Receive confirmation: You'll get an SMS from M-PESA confirming the payment.
  6. Minutes delivered: Your minutes are sent to your phone instantly.
✅ Why STK Push is best: No need to type Till numbers or amounts manually. Everything is automated, reducing errors and saving time.

Manual Till Payment

If you prefer to send money manually, you can use the standard M-PESA "Lipa na M-PESA" option:

  1. Open M-PESA: Go to the M-PESA menu on your phone.
  2. Select "Lipa na M-PESA": Choose this option from the menu.
  3. Select "Pay Bill": This is the option for paying to a business.
  4. Enter Business Number: Type 1621614 (Bingwa Data Till).
  5. Enter Account Number: Type your phone number (the one where you want minutes delivered).
  6. Enter Amount: Type the total amount for your bundle.
  7. Enter your M-PESA PIN: Confirm the transaction.
  8. Send confirmation: Forward the M-PESA confirmation message to our WhatsApp number (0743731749) so we can process your order.
Important: After making a manual payment, always send us the confirmation SMS so we can verify and deliver your minutes immediately.

After Payment – What Happens?

Once you complete your M-PESA payment, here's what to expect:

  • Confirmation SMS: You'll receive an SMS from M-PESA confirming the payment.
  • Bundle delivery: Your minutes are sent to your phone within seconds (STK Push) or within 2-3 minutes (manual payment).
  • Delivery confirmation: You'll receive an SMS from Bingwa Data confirming your minutes have been added.
  • Balance update: Your minute balance is updated immediately. You can check via USSD or your account portal.
  • Support: If you don't receive your minutes within 5 minutes, contact us on WhatsApp at 0743731749.
