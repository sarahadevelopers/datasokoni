What is Okoa Jahazi?

Okoa Jahazi (Swahili for "rescue the ship") is Safaricom’s micro‑credit service for prepaid customers. When your airtime balance hits zero or falls below a certain threshold, you can borrow a small amount – airtime, data, or SMS bundles – to stay connected.

📌 Key facts: The loan amount is automatically deducted from your next top‑up, plus a 10% service fee. It’s available 24/7, and you can access it via USSD, the MySafaricom App, or SMS.

Eligibility & Requirements

Not everyone qualifies immediately. Here are the conditions to borrow Okoa Jahazi:

  • Active Safaricom line for at least 3 months.
  • Minimum Ksh 20 weekly top‑up in the past 30 days.
  • No existing unpaid Okoa loan.
  • Your balance must be below Ksh 5 to trigger the offer (though you can borrow anytime).
  • Good repayment history – repeated defaults lower your eligibility.

⚠️ Tip: If you’ve recently switched to Safaricom, you may need to use the line actively for a few months before Okoa becomes available. Top up regularly to build your credit profile.

How to Borrow Okoa Jahazi (3 Methods)

Method 1: USSD (*544#)

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 4 (Okoa Jahazi).
  3. Choose Option 1 (Borrow Airtime) or Borrow Data/SMS.
  4. Select the amount (e.g., Ksh 10, 20, 50, 100, or up to your limit).
  5. Confirm the transaction. The credit is added instantly.

Method 2: MySafaricom App

  • Open the app and tap “Okoa” on the dashboard.
  • Choose “Borrow” and select the amount.
  • Confirm with your PIN – the credit is applied immediately.

Method 3: SMS

  • Send “BORROW” to 544.
  • Reply with the amount you wish to borrow.
  • You’ll receive a confirmation SMS.

How to Check Your Okoa Balance

Knowing your outstanding loan and remaining limit helps you plan your finances.

  • USSD: Dial *544# → Option 4 (Okoa) → Option 2 (Check Balance).
  • SMS: Send “BALANCE” to 544. You’ll receive a reply with the amount owed and your available limit.
  • MySafaricom App: Navigate to the Okoa section; the balance is displayed on the screen.

💡 Pro tip: Check your balance after every top‑up to confirm the loan has been deducted. If the balance doesn’t update, wait a few minutes or contact Safaricom customer care.

Repayment – How & When to Pay

Okoa Jahazi is designed to be self‑settling:

  • Repayment period: 7 days from the date of borrowing.
  • Service fee: 10% of the borrowed amount (e.g., borrow Ksh 50, pay Ksh 55).
  • Automatic deduction: The total amount (loan + fee) is deducted from your next airtime top‑up, whether it’s Ksh 10 or Ksh 500.
  • No action required: Simply top up your line – the system takes care of the rest.

⚠️ Important: If you don’t top up within 7 days, the loan remains pending. Your ability to borrow again is blocked, and the deduction will happen on your next top‑up, even if it’s weeks later.

Consequences of Non-Repayment

Failing to repay Okoa Jahazi can have several effects:

  • Immediate block: You cannot borrow again until the debt is cleared.
  • Reduced future limit: Even after clearing, your maximum borrowing amount may drop significantly.
  • Long‑term restriction: Repeated defaults can lead to permanent loss of Okoa access.
  • Impact on other services: Your Fuliza or KCB M‑PESA limit could also be affected, as Safaricom shares credit data internally.

📌 Note: Okoa Jahazi is not reported to CRB (Credit Reference Bureau). So it won’t affect your bank loans, but it can hurt your telecom creditworthiness.

Understanding Your Okoa Limit

Your borrowing limit isn’t fixed – it grows with responsible usage.

  • Starting limit: Usually Ksh 10–50 for new users.
  • Maximum limit: Up to Ksh 500 for well‑established customers.
  • How to increase it: Borrow small amounts and repay on time. Regular top‑ups and consistent usage also help.
  • How to lose it: Late repayments, defaults, or infrequent use can decrease your limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Okoa Jahazi?

Okoa Jahazi is Safaricom's emergency credit service that allows prepaid customers to borrow airtime, data, or SMS when their balance is low. The loan is automatically deducted from the next top-up, with a 10% service fee.

How do I borrow Okoa Jahazi?

Dial *544# and select Okoa Jahazi, then choose Borrow Airtime. Select the amount (Ksh 10–500 depending on your limit) and confirm. You can also use the MySafaricom App or SMS 'BORROW' to 544.

How do I check my Okoa Jahazi balance?

Dial *544# and select Okoa Jahazi → Check Balance. You can also send 'BALANCE' to 544 via SMS or check the MySafaricom App. This shows your outstanding loan and remaining limit.

What happens if I don't repay Okoa Jahazi?

If you don't repay within 7 days, Safaricom restricts your ability to borrow again. The debt is automatically deducted from your next top-up. Repeated non‑repayment can permanently reduce your Okoa limit.
