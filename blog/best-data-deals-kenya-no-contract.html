Smart Tips for Data Users

Maximize your savings with these pro tips:

  • Use dual-SIM: Put Safaricom for voice and Airtel for data. You get the best of both worlds – reliable coverage + cheap data.
  • Buy in bulk: The 30-day bundles (like Airtel 14GB) always offer the best per-GB price. If you're a consistent user, these are your best friends.
  • Schedule downloads: Use night bundles for large updates, streaming, and downloads. You'll save daytime data for work and social media.
  • Monitor your usage: Most phones have built-in data trackers. Set warnings at 80% so you never run out unexpectedly.
  • Share with friends: Some bundles can be shared via hotspot or tethering. Split the cost with a friend to save even more.
