What is Sambaza Airtime?

Sambaza (Swahili for "share") is Safaricom's airtime transfer service. It allows postpaid and prepaid customers to send airtime from their balance to any other Safaricom number. The recipient receives the airtime instantly and can use it for calls, SMS, or data bundles.

📌 Key Fact: Sambaza works only between Safaricom numbers. You cannot send airtime to Airtel or Telkom lines using this service.

Method 1: Using USSD (*140#)

The USSD method is the fastest and most popular way to Sambaza. Here's how:

  1. Dial *140# on your Safaricom line and press send.
  2. Select Option 1 (Send Airtime).
  3. Enter the recipient's Safaricom phone number (without spaces, e.g., 0712345678).
  4. Enter the amount you want to send (between Ksh 5 and Ksh 5,000).
  5. Confirm the transaction. The system will show you the total cost (amount + fee).
  6. Enter your MPESA PIN (yes, it uses M-PESA PIN for security).
  7. You'll receive a confirmation SMS. The recipient also gets a notification.

Method 2: Using MySafaricom App

If you prefer a visual interface, the MySafaricom App is a great alternative:

  • Step 1: Open the MySafaricom App and log in with your PIN or biometrics.
  • Step 2: On the home dashboard, tap "Send Airtime" or "Sambaza".
  • Step 3: Enter the recipient's phone number and the amount.
  • Step 4: Review the fee and tap "Confirm".
  • Step 5: Enter your M-PESA PIN to complete the transaction.

💡 Pro Tip: The app saves your frequently contacted numbers, making repeat Sambaza even faster.

Method 3: Using SMS

For those who prefer text, there's an SMS option – though it's less commonly used:

  1. Open your SMS app and create a new message.
  2. Type: SAMB [AMOUNT] [PHONE_NUMBER]
    Example: SAMB 50 0712345678
  3. Send the SMS to 140.
  4. You'll receive a reply asking you to confirm the transaction.
  5. Reply with your M-PESA PIN to complete.

⚠️ Note: Standard SMS rates apply to this service, so ensure you have at least 1 shilling of real airtime to send the message.

Can You Sambaza Bundles or Minutes?

Short answer: No. Safaricom's Sambaza service only allows transfer of airtime, not data bundles or voice minutes. However, the recipient can use the received airtime to buy their own bundles via USSD or from platforms like Bingwa Data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I Sambaza airtime on Safaricom?

You can Sambaza airtime using three methods: dial *140# and follow the prompts, use the MySafaricom App and select 'Send Airtime', or send an SMS with 'SAMB AMOUNT PHONE' (e.g., SAMB 50 0712345678) to 140.

What is the Sambaza USSD code?

The USSD code for Sambaza airtime on Safaricom is *140#. Dial it and select the option to send airtime, then enter the recipient's phone number and the amount.

Can I Sambaza minutes on Safaricom?

No. Safaricom does not allow Sambaza for minutes. You can only transfer airtime (which the recipient can use to buy minutes or data). Some networks like Airtel also only allow airtime transfer.

What are the fees for Sambaza airtime?

Safaricom charges a small transaction fee for Sambaza, which is deducted from the sender's balance. For example, sending Ksh 50 may cost Ksh 53 total (Ksh 50 + Ksh 3 fee). Check *140# for the exact fee before confirming.
