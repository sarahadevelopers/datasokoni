What is Airtel Okoa (Pewa)?

Airtel Okoa (officially branded as Pewa) is Airtel Kenya's emergency credit service. When your airtime balance runs low, you can borrow a small amount – usually between Ksh 10 and Ksh 500 – to make calls, send SMS, or buy data. The loan is automatically deducted from your next top-up, with a 10% service fee.

📌 Key fact: Airtel gives you 14 days to repay – twice as long as Safaricom's Okoa Jahazi (7 days). This gives you more breathing room.

Why You Need to Pay on Time

  • Keep borrowing privileges: If you don't repay, you won't be able to borrow again until the debt is cleared.
  • Maintain your limit: Late repayments can permanently reduce your borrowing limit.
  • Avoid service restrictions: Airtel may block your ability to access other services like bundles or premium SMS.
  • Build trust: Consistent on-time repayment can increase your Okoa limit over time.

How to Pay Airtel Okoa via M-PESA

There are three official ways to repay your Airtel Okoa loan using M-PESA. Here's how each works:

Method 1: Top Up Your Airtel Line with M-PESA (Recommended)

This is the easiest and most common method – the loan is deducted automatically.

  1. Open the M-PESA menu on your phone (dial *334# or use the app).
  2. Select "Lipa na M-PESA" → "Buy Airtime".
  3. Choose "Airtel" as the network.
  4. Enter the amount you want to top up (must be at least the loan amount + fees).
  5. Enter your M-PESA PIN and confirm.
  6. You'll receive a confirmation SMS from M-PESA and Airtel.
  7. The Okoa loan will be automatically deducted from the airtime you just purchased. The remaining airtime will be available for your use.

💡 Example: If you owe Ksh 55 (Ksh 50 borrowed + Ksh 5 fee), top up at least Ksh 55. The system will deduct Ksh 55, leaving any extra amount on your line.

Method 2: Via USSD (*544#) – Direct Repayment

You can also trigger a repayment directly without buying airtime first.

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 4 (Pewa/Okoa).
  3. Choose "Repay Loan" or "Settle Balance".
  4. You'll see your outstanding amount. Select the option to pay via M-PESA.
  5. Enter your M-PESA PIN to confirm the payment.
  6. You'll receive a confirmation SMS from both Airtel and M-PESA.

Method 3: Via MyAirtel App

  • Open the MyAirtel App and log in.
  • Go to "Pewa/Okoa" section.
  • Tap on "Repay" and choose M-PESA as the payment method.
  • Enter your M-PESA PIN to confirm.
  • The loan will be cleared instantly, and you'll receive a confirmation.

Fees and Charges

Understanding the costs helps you plan your repayment:

  • Service fee: 10% of the borrowed amount. Borrow Ksh 50 → repay Ksh 55.
  • No additional M-PESA charges: Airtel does not charge extra for the repayment transaction itself.
  • No interest for late repayment: However, you may lose access to future borrowing.

What Happens If You Don't Pay?

  • Service restriction: You won't be able to borrow again until the debt is cleared.
  • Reduced future limit: Even after repayment, your maximum borrowing amount may be lowered.
  • Potential for permanent ban: Repeated defaults can lead to permanent removal from the Okoa service.
  • No CRB reporting: Airtel Okoa is not reported to Credit Reference Bureaus, so it won't affect your credit score. But it does affect your Airtel creditworthiness.

⚠️ Important: If you're unable to repay on time, try to top up as soon as possible – the automatic deduction will work even if it's past the 14‑day window. The sooner you clear it, the faster you regain full access.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay my Airtel Okoa loan using M-PESA?

The easiest way is to simply top up your Airtel line with airtime using M-PESA – the Okoa loan is automatically deducted. Alternatively, you can dial *544# and select the repayment option, then pay via M-PESA. Some users also use the MyAirtel App to make repayments.

What is the Airtel Okoa repayment period?

Airtel gives you 14 days to repay your Okoa (Pewa) loan. This is double the 7 days that Safaricom offers for Okoa Jahazi. If you don't repay within 14 days, your ability to borrow again is restricted.

Are there fees for repaying Airtel Okoa?

Airtel charges a 10% service fee on the borrowed amount. So if you borrow Ksh 50, you'll repay Ksh 55. This fee is automatically deducted when you top up your line. No extra M-PESA transaction fees are charged for the repayment itself.

Can I still buy bundles if I have an outstanding Airtel Okoa loan?

Yes. You can still buy bundles from Airtel using the remaining airtime, or you can buy bundles from platforms like Bingwa Data using M-PESA. However, you won't be able to borrow again until the loan is cleared.
