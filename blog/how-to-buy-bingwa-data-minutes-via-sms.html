SMS Format & Examples

Keep your SMS simple and clear. Here is the standard format:

[Bundle Name] to [Phone Number]
Example:
45 minutes to 0712345678
Or if you're ordering for yourself:
120 minutes

Important: If you don't specify a phone number, we will deliver the minutes to the number you're sending the SMS from.

Examples of valid SMS orders:
  • 45 minutes – deliver to my number
  • 120 minutes to 0712345678 – deliver to another number
  • 300 minutes for 0722334455 – alternative format
  • 600 minutes – deliver to my number

Step-by-Step Process

Follow these steps to order minutes via SMS:

  1. Compose a new SMS: Open your phone's messaging app.
  2. Enter the recipient: Type 0743731749 as the destination number.
  3. Write your order: Type your bundle request (e.g., "45 minutes" or "120 minutes to 0712345678").
  4. Send the message: Press send.
  5. Wait for our reply: We will respond with a payment link or STK Push request.
  6. Complete payment: Follow the payment instructions sent to you.
  7. Receive minutes: Your minutes will be delivered instantly after payment confirmation.
✅ Pro Tip: Save the number 0743731749 in your contacts as "Bingwa Data SMS" for quick access next time.
