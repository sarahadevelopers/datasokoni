Can You Sambaza Minutes?

No. Safaricom's Sambaza service only allows the transfer of airtime, not voice minutes or data bundles. Whether you have purchased a voice bundle or have accumulated free minutes, you cannot send them to another Safaricom number.

⚠️ Important: This applies to all networks in Kenya. Neither Safaricom, Airtel, nor Telkom allows the transfer of minutes or data. Only airtime can be Sambaza'd.

Why Can't You Transfer Minutes?

  • Technical limitation: Minutes are tied to your specific line and cannot be decoupled from your account.
  • Network policy: Telecoms want you to buy airtime, which is the only transferable currency. Minutes are a usage product, not a transferable asset.
  • Fraud prevention: Allowing minute transfers could lead to abuse and fraudulent activities.
  • Commercial reasons: Networks earn more when you buy airtime to Sambaza rather than transferring existing minutes.

Alternatives to Sambaza Minutes

Even though you can't transfer minutes directly, here are practical ways to help someone make calls:

Alternative 1: Sambaza Airtime (Best Option)

This is the simplest solution. Instead of trying to send minutes, Sambaza airtime to your friend. They can then use the airtime to buy their own voice bundles or make pay-as-you-go calls.

Alternative 2: Buy and Share a Bundle

You can't share a bundle you've already purchased, but you can buy a bundle for someone else using your airtime or M-PESA:

  • Using airtime: If you have airtime on your line, you can dial *544# and buy a bundle for the recipient's number. Most Safaricom and Airtel bundles allow you to purchase for another line.

Alternative 3: Use WhatsApp or VoIP

If both of you have smartphones and data, you can make free calls using WhatsApp, Telegram, or other VoIP apps. This uses data, not minutes, and can be cheaper in the long run.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I Sambaza minutes on Safaricom?

No. Safaricom does not allow Sambaza for minutes. You can only transfer airtime, not voice minutes or data bundles. However, you can buy airtime and send it to someone so they can purchase their own minutes or bundles.

How do I share minutes on Safaricom?

There is no direct way to share minutes on Safaricom. The only way is to buy airtime and Sambaza it to the person who needs to make calls. They can then use the airtime to buy voice minutes via USSD.

Can I buy minutes and give to someone else?

Yes. You can buy a voice bundle using your airtime, but the bundle is tied to your line – it cannot be transferred. The best alternative is to Sambaza airtime so the recipient can buy their own minutes.

What is the cheapest way to make calls in Kenya?

Buying voice bundles from Safaricom (like 45 minutes for Ksh 19) is the cheapest way. At just Ksh 0.42 per minute, it's far cheaper than normal pay-as-you-go calling. You can also use WhatsApp or other VoIP apps over data.
