What is Airtime to Cash?

Airtime to Cash is an official Safaricom service that allows prepaid customers to convert a portion of their airtime balance into M‑PESA money. The service is available 24/7 and works instantly.

📌 Key fact: This is the only official way to turn Safaricom airtime into cash. Be cautious of third‑party apps or individuals offering to convert airtime – they may be scams.

Step-by-Step Guide to Converting Airtime

Follow these simple steps to convert your airtime to M‑PESA cash:

  1. Dial *544# on your Safaricom line and press send.
  2. Select the "Airtime to Cash" option from the main menu. (Note: This is usually option 7 or 8, depending on your menu version.)
  3. Enter the amount you want to convert (between Ksh 10 and Ksh 1,000).
  4. You'll see a summary showing the amount you'll receive and the fees charged.
  5. Enter your M‑PESA PIN to confirm the transaction.
  6. You'll receive a confirmation SMS from Safaricom. The cash will be credited to your M‑PESA account instantly.

  • Daily limit: Maximum of Ksh 2,000 per day.
  • Eligibility: Only available to Safaricom prepaid customers.

⚠️ Important: The fees are deducted from the amount you convert. So if you convert Ksh 100, you only receive Ksh 85 in M‑PESA.

Alternative Ways to Use Your Airtime

If you don't want to pay the conversion fees, here are some alternatives:

  • Sambaza to a friend: If a friend needs airtime and can send you cash via M‑PESA, you can Sambaza the airtime to them. You'll pay a small Sambaza fee (Ksh 1–5), which is much lower than the 10% conversion fee.
  • Buy bundles for yourself: Use the airtime to purchase Safaricom data or voice bundles directly via USSD.
  • Buy bundles for others: If you have friends or family who need data, you can buy bundles for them using your airtime and ask them to send you cash via M‑PESA.
  • Save it for emergencies: Keep the airtime for when you really need to make a call or buy emergency data.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Safaricom airtime to M-PESA cash?

Dial *544# on your Safaricom line, select the 'Airtime to Cash' option, enter the amount you want to convert (between Ksh 10 and Ksh 1,000), enter your M-PESA PIN, and confirm. The cash will be credited to your M-PESA account instantly.

What are the fees for converting airtime to cash on Safaricom?

Safaricom charges a 10% transaction fee plus a Ksh 5 service charge. For example, converting Ksh 100 airtime to cash will cost Ksh 15 in fees (Ksh 10 + Ksh 5), so you'll receive Ksh 85 in M-PESA.

What is the daily limit for Safaricom airtime to cash conversion?

The minimum amount you can convert is Ksh 10, and the maximum per transaction is Ksh 1,000. The daily limit for this service is Ksh 2,000.

Yes! Instead of paying high fees to convert airtime to cash, you can use that airtime to buy bundles directly from Safaricom, or you can sell it to trusted friends. However, the best value is often to keep the airtime for emergencies, or use it to buy cheap bundles from platforms like Bingwa Data.
