What is a Bundles Balance?

A bundles balance shows you how much of your purchased data, voice minutes, or SMS you have left before the bundle expires. Checking your balance regularly helps you:

  • Avoid running out of data unexpectedly.
  • Plan your usage better.
  • Know when to buy a new bundle.
  • Track your spending and avoid waste.

📌 Key fact: Bundles have different validity periods – some last hours, others days or weeks. Always check the expiry date when you check your balance.

How to Check Safaricom Data Balance

Safaricom offers multiple ways to check your data balance:

Method 1: USSD (*544#)

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 2 (Data Bundles).
  3. Select Option 1 (Check Balance).
  4. Your screen will display your remaining data in MB or GB.

⚡ Quick Reference (Safaricom Data)

USSD Code*544#
Menu PathOption 2 → Option 1
Shortcut*544*2#

Method 2: MySafaricom App

  • Open the MySafaricom App and log in.
  • Your data balance is displayed on the home dashboard.
  • Tap on it to see a detailed breakdown of all active data bundles.

Method 3: SMS

  • Send "DATA" to 544.
  • You'll receive an SMS with your remaining data balance.

How to Check Safaricom Minutes Balance

Voice minutes on Safaricom can be checked using these methods:

Method 1: USSD (*544#)

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 3 (Voice Bundles) or Option 2 (Data Bundles) depending on your menu.
  3. Select "Check Balance" or "My Bundles".
  4. Your remaining minutes will be displayed.

⚡ Quick Reference (Safaricom Minutes)

USSD Code*544#
Menu PathOption 3 → Check Balance
AlternativeMySafaricom App → Voice Bundles

Method 2: MySafaricom App

  • Open the app and go to "Voice Bundles".
  • Your remaining minutes and expiry date will be shown.

How to Check Safaricom SMS Balance

Safaricom SMS bundles are less common but still available. Here's how to check:

  • USSD: Dial *544# → Option 5 (SMS Bundles) → Check Balance.
  • MySafaricom App: Navigate to the SMS bundles section.
  • SMS: Send "SMS" to 544.

⚠️ Note: SMS bundles are less popular now due to WhatsApp and other messaging apps. Most users no longer need dedicated SMS bundles.

How to Check Airtel Bundles Balance

Airtel's menu is slightly different. Here's how to check your balances:

Data Balance

  1. Dial *544# and press send.
  2. Select Option 2 (Data Bundles).
  3. Select Option 1 (Check Balance).
  4. Your remaining data will be displayed in MB or GB.

⚡ Quick Reference (Airtel Data)

USSD Code*544#
Menu PathOption 2 → Option 1
Shortcut*544*2#

Voice Minutes & SMS Balance (Airtel)

  • When you check your data balance on Airtel (*544# → Option 2 → Option 1), it also displays your remaining voice minutes and SMS balance.
  • Alternatively, use the MyAirtel App to see all balances in one place.

💡 Pro Tip: Airtel combines data, minutes, and SMS balances in one menu. So checking data balance automatically shows you everything!

SMS Balance *544# Option 5 → Check Balance
Airtel Data Balance *544# Option 2 → Option 1

Using Apps & SMS to Check Balances

If USSD isn't convenient, here are the alternatives:

MySafaricom App (Safaricom)

  • Download from Play Store or App Store.
  • Log in with your phone number and PIN.
  • Your balances are displayed on the dashboard.
  • Tap on each bundle to see details and expiry dates.

MyAirtel App (Airtel)

  • Download from Play Store or App Store.
  • Log in with your Airtel number.
  • Your data, minutes, and SMS balances are shown on the home screen.

SMS Balance Check (Both Networks)

  • Safaricom: Send "DATA", "MINUTES", or "SMS" to 544.
  • Airtel: Send "BALANCE" to 544 – you'll receive a summary of all balances.

⚠️ Note: Standard SMS rates apply to balance check messages. If you have zero airtime, you may not be able to send the SMS.

Troubleshooting – What If You Can't Check Your Balance?

  • USSD not working: Check your network signal. Move to a location with better coverage and try again.
  • Menu option missing: Different versions of the USSD menu may have different options. Look for "My Bundles" or "Active Subscriptions" as alternatives.
  • No balance displayed: You may not have any active bundles. Consider buying a new bundle from Bingwa Data.
  • App issues: Update your app or reinstall it. Also, check that you have a stable internet connection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check my Safaricom data balance?

Dial *544# and select Option 2 (Data Bundles) then Option 1 (Check Balance). You can also dial *544*2# as a shortcut, or use the MySafaricom App to see your data usage.

How do I check my Airtel data balance?

Dial *544# and select Option 2 (Data Bundles) then Option 1 (Check Balance). You can also dial *544*2# or use the MyAirtel App to see your remaining data and minutes.

How do I check my voice minutes balance?

On Safaricom, dial *544# and select Voice Bundles then Check Balance. On Airtel, dial *544# and select Data Bundles then Check Balance – your minutes balance will be displayed alongside data. You can also check via the MySafaricom or MyAirtel apps.

Can I check my bundles balance without using USSD?

Yes. You can use the MySafaricom App (Safaricom) or MyAirtel App (Airtel) to view all your balances. You can also send 'BALANCE' to 544 via SMS to receive a summary of your data, minutes, and SMS balances.
