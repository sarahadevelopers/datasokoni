Are you looking for a way to earn extra income? Bingwa Data offers a lucrative agent program that allows you to resell our affordable bundles and earn commissions on every sale.

This guide explains everything you need to know about becoming a Bingwa Data agent – from requirements and registration to commissions and benefits. Start your journey as a reseller today!

Why Become a Bingwa Data Agent?

  • Earn passive income: Make money every time you sell a bundle.
  • No inventory needed: No physical products to store – just sell digital bundles.
  • Work from anywhere: Sell from your phone or computer.
  • Flexible hours: Set your own schedule and work at your own pace.
  • Growing market: Kenya's demand for affordable data and minutes is increasing daily.

Agent Requirements

Becoming a Bingwa Data agent is simple. Here's what you need:

  • Valid M-PESA number – for receiving commissions and making payments.
  • Smartphone or basic phone – to communicate with customers and our team.
  • Active WhatsApp account – for receiving updates and support.
  • Willingness to promote – basic marketing and sales skills (or willingness to learn).
  • No prior experience required – we provide full training and support.
Note: There is no registration fee to become an agent. It's completely free to join.

Commission Structure

Our commission structure is designed to reward your sales efforts. The more you sell, the higher your commission rate:

Agent Tier Monthly Sales (Ksh) Commission Rate
Bronze 0 – 49,999 5%
Silver 50,000 – 199,999 8%
Gold 200,000 – 499,999 12%
Platinum 500,000+ 15%

Example: If you sell Ksh 100,000 worth of bundles in a month, you earn 8% commission = Ksh 8,000.

💪 Bonus: Top-performing agents also receive monthly bonuses and exclusive promotional materials.

How to Register as an Agent

Follow these steps to become a Bingwa Data agent:

  1. Visit the agent registration page: Go to datasokoni.com and click on the "Become an Agent" link.
  2. Fill in the registration form: Provide your full name, phone number, email address, and location.
  3. Submit your application: Click the "Apply Now" button.
  4. Receive confirmation: Our team will review your application and contact you via WhatsApp or email within 24 hours.
  5. Activate your agent account: Once approved, you'll receive instructions on how to access your agent dashboard.
  6. Start selling: Share your unique agent link with customers and earn commissions on every sale.
Important: Ensure you provide accurate contact details so we can reach you for activation and training.

Agent Benefits

As a Bingwa Data agent, you'll enjoy the following benefits:

  • Exclusive agent prices: Get bundles at discounted rates for your own use.
  • Marketing support: We provide promotional materials, banners, and tips to help you sell.
  • Dedicated support: A dedicated agent support team is available via WhatsApp to help you with any issues.
  • Early access: Get early access to new bundles and promotions before they're released to the public.
  • Recognition: Top agents are featured on our website and social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a Bingwa Data agent?

To become an agent, complete the online registration form, provide your details, and submit your application. Our team will review and activate your agent account.

What are the requirements to be an agent?

You need a valid M-PESA number, a smartphone or basic phone, and a willingness to promote Bingwa Data bundles to your network. No prior experience required.

How much commission do agents earn?

Commission rates start from 5% of the bundle price and can go up to 15% depending on your sales volume and agent tier.

How do I get paid my commissions?

Commissions are paid directly to your M-PESA number on a weekly or monthly basis, depending on the agent tier you qualify for.

Is there a fee to become an agent?

No, becoming a Bingwa Data agent is completely free. There are no registration or maintenance fees.
